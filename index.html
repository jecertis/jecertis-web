What is SOC 2 audit as a service?

SOC 2 audit as a service means an audit team handles the engagement from scoping through findings: scoping it, testing your controls against the SOC 2 trust services criteria, and walking through findings with you. The same approach applies to ISO/IEC 27001, the information security management standard. We are building this service with independent consultant-auditors we engage.

The formal path for how a SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate ultimately gets issued is still being worked out, so we're not asserting a specific structure for that here. See the questions below for more.

Questions about SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits in India

What does SOC 2 audit as a service from JeCertis cover?

It covers scoping the engagement, testing your controls against the framework, and working through findings with your team. JeCertis is building this delivery with independent consultant-auditors it engages, and engagements are scoped with you. SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001 are our primary focus today.

Who is JeCertis for?

Startups and growing companies in India, typically 50 to 500 people, that need a SOC 2 or ISO/IEC 27001 audit, often because a customer or investor has asked for one. The usual contacts are a CTO, co-founder or head of compliance.

What is SOC 2 readiness for a startup?

SOC 2 readiness is the work done before the audit: understanding which trust services criteria apply, checking which controls already exist, and finding gaps while there is still time to close them. It lets a founder see what an auditor will look for before the audit starts.

How long does a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 engagement take?

It depends on scope, how mature your existing controls are and, for a SOC 2 Type 2 report, the observation period agreed with the auditor. We discuss timeline after scoping, not before, so we do not publish a fixed duration.

Who signs the SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate?

The formal path for how a SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate is issued is still being worked out, and we do not state a structure here. Ask us directly and we will tell you where it stands before you commit to anything.

Does JeCertis offer a DPDP compliance audit in India?

Not yet at the same maturity as SOC 2 and ISO 27001. DPDP Act 2023 assessments are on our roadmap as the next framework after those two. If you need one, tell us your timeline and we will say plainly whether we can help.

What does a JeCertis engagement conclude about my company?

An engagement produces audit work, findings and supporting evidence about your controls against the framework in scope. It is not a legal conclusion, JeCertis does not certify your company, and we do not promise any particular outcome. You receive a record of what was tested.
