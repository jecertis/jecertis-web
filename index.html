What this is

JeCertis is hiring consultant-auditors onto our own team to deliver audit and assessment work: scoping the engagement, testing controls, and working through findings with the company being audited or assessed.

The formal path for how a SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate ultimately gets issued is still being worked out, so we're not asserting a specific structure for that here.
